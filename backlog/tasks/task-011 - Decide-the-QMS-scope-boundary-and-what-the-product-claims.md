## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Clauses 4, 5, 6, 9.2, 9.3 and 10.3 - context of the organisation, quality policy, objectives, internal audit, management review and the improvement programme - are held nowhere, and the application offers no hook for them. There is no CAPA register, no audit finding record and no corrective action workflow; 10.2 currently stops at a reason recorded against an offcut or a goods receipt.

That is a scope boundary rather than a defect. It needs stating plainly because the product supports a fabricator's QMS for clause 8 and touches nothing else, and the commercial risk is describing it as more than that.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
